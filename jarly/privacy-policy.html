Jarly Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 31, 2026

This policy explains how Jarly: Mood Tracker & Diary ("Jarly", "the app"), developed by Faraz Ahmed Abir (FarazInc, "we", "us"), handles your information. In short: your diary stays on your device. We do not run servers that collect, store or read your entries.

1. Information you create in the app

Moods, emojis, notes, tags, mood ratings, photos, videos and voice notes that you add to entries are stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. We never receive, upload or have access to this content.

App settings (such as your jar theme, reminder time and achievements) are also stored locally on your device.

2. Device permissions

  • Microphone – only when you record a voice note. Recordings are saved on your device.
  • Photos, videos and camera – only when you choose to attach media to an entry.
  • Motion sensors – used to let the emojis in your jar move when you tilt your phone. Sensor data is not stored or shared.
  • Notifications – used for optional daily reminders that you can turn off at any time.
  • Biometrics / PIN – used for the optional app lock. Fingerprint and face data are handled by your device's operating system and are never available to us. Your PIN is stored on your device only as a salted hash.

3. Backups and sharing

If you export a backup or share a mood card, the app creates a file and hands it to the destination you choose (for example, your files app or another app). Where that file goes is controlled by you and that destination's own privacy policy. We do not receive a copy.

Your device's own system backup (such as Google or iCloud device backup) may include app data, depending on your device settings.

4. Advertising

Jarly is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, device and app information, and ad interactions. This collection is done by Google, not by us, and is governed by how Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Where required (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), the app asks for your consent before showing personalised ads, using Google's consent tool. You can change your choice at any time from the app's Settings (Privacy options). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device settings.

Your diary content is never used for advertising and is never shared with Google.

5. What we do not do

  • We do not require an account or collect your name, email or phone number.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not use analytics or tracking tools of our own.

6. Data retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. You can delete individual entries in the app, or remove everything by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app. Because we hold no copy, we cannot recover deleted data, so export a backup first if you want to keep it.

7. Children

Jarly is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided information through the app's ads, contact us and we will help.

8. Security

Your data is protected by your device's security and the app's private storage. You can add a PIN or biometric lock in Settings. No method of storage is 100% secure, so keep your device protected with a screen lock.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
